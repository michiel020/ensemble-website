Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · November 22, 2026

Ensemble (Michiel Alkemade and Reinout d’Engelbronner, Amsterdam) runs this website. This policy explains what the site collects, why, and what you can choose.

What this website collects

  • Hosting. Netlify serves this site. Like any web server, Netlify sees your IP address and browser details in order to show the page.
  • Fonts and Google Tag Manager. The fonts come from Google Fonts, and Google Tag Manager loads the services below. Google sees your IP address when this happens.
  • Counting visits, without cookies. PostHog (servers in the EU) counts visits, pages viewed and clicks. As long as you don’t accept cookies, PostHog sets no cookies and stores no visitor ID in your browser. PostHog does not store your IP address.
  • Only with your consent: cookies. If you click “Yes, please”, Google Analytics (Google Ireland) starts measuring and sets the cookies _ga and _ga_*, and PostHog stores a random visitor ID in a cookie (ph_…) and in your browser’s storage. This lets them recognize a returning browser. Sharing this data with Google for other purposes is turned off. Google may process data outside the EU, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Your choice is stored in your browser for 12 months, so we don’t ask on every visit.
  • Email. If you email us, we only use your message to reply.

Why

We only use these numbers to see how the site is used and to improve it. We don’t sell them and don’t use them for advertising. We only set cookies with your consent. Counting visits without cookies is based on our legitimate interest: knowing whether the site gets visitors.

What you can choose

You can change your choice at any time: open the cookie settings, or use the link at the bottom of every page. If you decline, the Google Analytics and PostHog cookies are removed.

You can ask which data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted, via hallo@withensemble.nl. If you feel we don’t handle your data properly, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

When the website changes, we update this policy. The date at the top shows which version applies.
